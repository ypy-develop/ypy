# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that address; it applies to every space of the project
(issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel) and to anyone who
represents the project in public.

In short: be respectful, welcome people of every background and experience,
give and accept feedback with care, take responsibility for mistakes, and
focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks, publishing others' private information and similar conduct are not
acceptable.
